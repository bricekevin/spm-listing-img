A small Wi-Fi screen on a 3D-printed stand that shows one thing you would otherwise keep opening an app for, and keeps it up to date by itself.

How they work

  • Plug in, join it from your phone, and choose what it shows. A minute or two.
  • It connects straight to your Wi-Fi. No computer, app or subscription.
  • Same hardware across the family: 2 inch color screen, three buttons, USB-C.
  • A choice of stand finishes, and battery options if you want it cordless.
  • Each one updates itself over Wi-Fi as we add features.
