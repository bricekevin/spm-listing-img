About a minute, on your phone. No app, no account, no subscription.

  1. Plug it in. Use the USB-C cable in the box and any phone charger. The screen shows a code and a Wi-Fi name that starts with Sports Meter-.
  2. Scan the code with your phone camera, or open Wi-Fi settings and join the Sports Meter-XXXXXX network. The setup page opens by itself. If it does not, open a browser and go to 192.168.4.1.
  3. Pick your teams. Choose a league, then a team. Add up to six, from any mix of football, basketball, baseball, hockey and soccer, pro or college.
  4. Choose your home Wi-Fi and type its password, then tap Save. The network must be 2.4 GHz (most home routers are both; guest and 5 GHz-only networks will not work).
  5. Done. The device restarts and your first team appears in about 30 seconds.

New here? See what Sports Meter does.

What the screen shows

All weekSeason record, place in the standings, and the next game in your local time.
Game dayThe next-game card lights up in your team's colors.
During a gameLive score with the clock and period. Football adds down and distance; baseball adds the count, outs and runners.
AfterThe final score, then back to the record and the next game.

With several teams, they take turns on screen. A live game takes over until it ends.

Buttons

A (left)Tap: next team. Hold: status screen (Wi-Fi, version, last update).
B (middle)Tap: brightness. Hold 2 seconds: change teams.
C (right)Tap: screen off / on. Hold 5 seconds, and keep holding through the red screen: erase everything and start setup again.

Change teams later

Hold B for 2 seconds. The screen shows a web address. Open it on a phone or computer that is on the same Wi-Fi as the Sports Meter, pick your teams and save. Your Wi-Fi settings are kept.

If something is not right

The setup page did not open

Stay joined to the Sports Meter-XXXXXX network, open a browser and type 192.168.4.1. If your phone says the network has no internet, choose to stay connected.

It says "WiFi connect failed"

The password was mistyped or the network is 5 GHz only. Hold C for 5 seconds (keep holding through the red screen) and run setup again.

I picked the wrong team

Hold B for 2 seconds and use the address on the screen. Nothing else is changed.

The score is a little behind my TV

Scores usually trail the live action by under a minute. Streams are often further behind than that.

New Wi-Fi, moving house, or giving it away

Hold C for 5 seconds and keep holding through the red screen. That erases the Wi-Fi and teams and returns the device to setup.

The change-teams address will not load

The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi network as the device, not on mobile data or a guest network.
