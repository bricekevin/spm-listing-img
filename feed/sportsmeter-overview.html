Sports Meter

A small always-on desk display that follows your teams over Wi-Fi: the record and standings all week, the next game in your local time, and the live score while they play.

Any team, any sport, up to six at once

One Sports Meter follows up to six teams at the same time, from any mix of football, basketball, baseball, hockey and soccer, pro or college. They take turns on screen, and a live game takes over until it ends. The team in these photos is only an example: you choose your own teams at setup and can change them whenever you like. Other photos here show more teams and sports on the same device.

What it shows

All week

Season record, place in the standings and the next game in your local time.

Game day

The next-game card lights up in your team's colors.

During the game

Live score with the clock and period. Football adds down and distance; baseball adds the count, outs and runners.

After

The final score, then back to the record and what is next.

See it in a room

How it works

  1. Plug it in. USB-C, any phone charger.
  2. Scan the code on the screen and choose your Wi-Fi and your teams. About a minute.
  3. That is it. No app, no account, no subscription. It updates itself over Wi-Fi.

Change teams any time from your phone, without setting up Wi-Fi again. The full walkthrough is in the setup guide.

Finishes and power

Eight stand finishes (Black, Blue, Red, Wood, Gold, Rainbow, Orange, Glow) and three ways to power it: USB-C, Standard Battery or All Day Battery.

Good to know

Needs 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Scores usually trail the live action by well under a minute. Updates itself over Wi-Fi. In the box: Sports Meter display, 3D-printed desk stand, USB-C cable, quick-start card.
