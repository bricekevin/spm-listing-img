sunsetscore.com

Sunset Score predicts sunset quality anywhere on Earth and gives it a simple score out of 100. The model is trained on thousands of real sunset photographs paired with the weather that made them.

What it does

A score out of 100

A 30 is a quiet grey evening. An 80 is the one you stop to watch.

Anywhere in the world

Check where you are, scout a spot across town, or plan a trip.

Sunset alerts

Pick your places and a minimum score. Most nights you hear nothing. That is the point.

Why the score

Cloud cover at each height, the western horizon and the sun's path, laid out.

Share what you saw

Post your sunset and rate it, next to what was forecast.

No account needed

Forecasts are free to check. Sign in only to post photos.

A closer look

On the web

No download needed: open sunsetscore.com in any browser, on a laptop or a phone.
