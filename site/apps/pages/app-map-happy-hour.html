Map Happy Hour puts every happy hour on one live map: green is on now, yellow is ending soon, teal starts later today. Tap a pin for the deals, the times and directions. Seventeen metro areas across eight western states, and growing.

What it does

On now

See which bars and restaurants are pouring right now, and how long is left.

Deals and times

Each venue's menu specials and its hours for every day of the week.

Directions

One tap from the pin to the route there.

Favourites

Save your spots and get told when their happy hour starts.

Seventeen metros

From San Diego and LA to Seattle, Phoenix, Albuquerque and Boise, eight states in all.

Kept up to date

Hours are checked and corrected, and anyone can suggest a new spot.
