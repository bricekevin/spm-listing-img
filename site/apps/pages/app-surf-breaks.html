Surf Breaks turns live buoy, swell, wind and tide data into one simple score for every break, so you can tell at a glance where it is worth going this morning.

What it does

Wave score

Live data, historical patterns, beach angle and weather, rolled into one number per break.

Live reports

Wave height, period and direction from NOAA buoys, plus tide and wind for each spot.

Beach cams

Live streams of the breaks you care about, next to the numbers.

Map and globe

Hundreds of spots in dozens of countries, on a map or a 3D globe.

Periscope

Hold up your phone at the beach and see the scores of nearby breaks in place.

Widgets

Your favourite break's score, swell and tide on your Home Screen.

A closer look

What’s new

  1. Oct 2026

    Surf spots worldwide

    From California only to 357 breaks in 27 countries, grouped by country, state and region, with search, sort and filters.

  2. Oct 2026

    Periscope

    Point your phone at the coast and the nearby breaks appear over the live camera, with their scores and how far away they are.

  3. Oct 2026

    89 live beach cams

    Every cam checked live and linked to its break, in 24 countries. Tap Watch live cam on any spot that has one.

  4. Oct 2026

    Smarter scores

    Points and reefs now score on their own swell window, beach angles were corrected, and wave height uses one consistent formula.

  5. Oct 2026

    Home Screen widget

    Your favourite break, or the nearest one: the score, a tide chart and the weather, without opening the app.

  6. Oct 2026

    Good-surf alerts

    Set the score that is worth a paddle and get a nudge when a favourite break reaches it.

  7. Oct 2026

    A new look

    Big photos of every break, a glass-style map with score-coloured pins, a side menu and an inbox.
