Engraved Slate

Natural slate, laser engraved. The stone turns pale where the laser touches it, so photos and line art come out crisp and permanent.

How they work

  • Coaster sets, serving tiles and large portraits.
  • Send a photo or a design and we engrave it; or choose one of ours.
  • Every piece of slate is a little different at the edges. That is the point.
  • The engraving is cut into the stone, so it does not peel.
