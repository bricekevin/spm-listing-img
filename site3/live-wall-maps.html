Live Wall Maps

A map you hang on the wall, with LEDs behind it that follow the real thing: where the trains are, which lifts are turning.

How they work

  • Hang it, plug it in, and join it to your Wi-Fi from your phone.
  • It fetches live status every minute or so and lights the map to match.
  • Laser-cut and assembled in Encinitas; each map is made for one place.
  • Nothing to run on a computer, and no subscription.
