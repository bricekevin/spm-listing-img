Custom Made

Send us a photo or a list of names and we make it for you, in wood or in slate. For anything bigger or stranger, tell us what you have in mind.

How they work

  • Order the piece, then send your photo or details; we confirm before we cut.
  • Made to order in Encinitas, so allow a little longer than for stock pieces.
  • Good photos make good pieces: sharp, well lit, face toward the camera.
  • One-off projects are welcome. We design, laser cut, engrave and 3D print.
