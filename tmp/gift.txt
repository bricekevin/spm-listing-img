<h2>Questions people ask</h2>
<details><summary>What is a good gift for a sports fan who has everything?</summary><p>A Sports Meter: a small Wi-Fi desk display that shows their team&#x27;s live score, record, standings and next game all season. It is not something most fans already own, it works with any team, and it needs no app or subscription.</p></details>
<details><summary>What if I do not know which team they follow?</summary><p>It does not matter which one you buy. Every Sports Meter follows any supported team, and the person chooses their own teams when they set it up. The team in the photos is only an example.</p></details>
<details><summary>Which sports and leagues does it cover?</summary><p>Football, basketball, baseball, hockey and soccer, pro and college: the NFL, NBA, WNBA, MLB, NHL, MLS, NWSL, college football, men&#x27;s and women&#x27;s college basketball, the Premier League, LaLiga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1 and Liga MX. More than 1,700 teams in 16 leagues.</p></details>
<details><summary>Can it follow more than one team?</summary><p>Yes. One Sports Meter follows up to six teams at once, from any mix of sports. They take turns on screen, and a live game takes over until it ends.</p></details>
<details><summary>Is there a subscription or an app?</summary><p>No. There is no subscription, no account and no app. It connects to home Wi-Fi and updates itself.</p></details>
<details><summary>How hard is it to set up?</summary><p>About a minute. Plug it in, scan the code on its screen with a phone, choose the home Wi-Fi and pick the teams. Teams can be changed later from a phone.</p></details>
<details><summary>How much does it cost?</summary><p>$119 to $149, depending on the battery option. It comes with a 3D-printed stand in a choice of colors, a USB-C cable and a quick-start card.</p></details>
<details><summary>Does it show live scores?</summary><p>Yes. During a game it shows the score, the clock and the period, usually well under a minute behind the live action. Football adds down and distance; baseball adds the count, outs and runners.</p></details>
<details><summary>What does it need to work?</summary><p>A 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, which almost every home router has, and USB-C power or its built-in battery.</p></details>
